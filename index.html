Settings

The IRS 2026 business rate is 72.5¢/mile (Jan–Jun) and 76¢/mile (Jul–Dec). The app fills this in automatically by trip date — but every field is editable.

Where is my data?

In this browser, on this computer, under localStorage. It is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser history/site data will delete it — so export CSVs (or a JSON backup) regularly.

Help

Getting started, step by step

  1. Set your rate. Open Settings and check the default rate. The app already suggests the IRS business rate that was in effect for each trip date.
  2. Add a trip. On Trips, enter the date, why you drove, and the miles. The rate is filled in automatically — change it if you like. Click Add trip.
  3. Add receipts. On Receipts, enter the date, vendor, amount, and a category.
  4. Check your month. The Summary tab shows trips, miles, mileage dollars, receipts by category, and a grand total — plus a whole-year table.
  5. Export or print. Download CSVs from the Summary tab, or use Print View for a clean report — print it on paper, save it as a PDF, or hand it to your accountant.

A few honest notes

  • This tool is a calculator and a notebook, not tax advice. Check current rates and rules at irs.gov or ask a tax professional.
  • Stored only on this computer, in this browser. If you use a different browser or device, that copy won't show your entries.
  • Backups matter: use the CSV export buttons, or make a JSON backup from Settings.
  • The mileage rate lookup only covers recent IRS rates (2024–2026). Older dates use the rate you type — which is still fully editable and recorded with the trip.

Keyboard shortcuts

Nothing exotic: Tab to move between fields, Enter to save a form. That's it.
